Character Abilities are passive abilities with great impact on gameplay, to make every char unique

# Liebhaber
## Ability: Crush
One random Entity becomes the Liebhabers Crush. 
As long as the Crush is alive, he recieves buffs. 
When the Entity that is the Crush dies, the Liebhaber rexieves a great buff for the rest of the room.
(if crush is the last one standing he siezes to be the crush)

Crush Buff:
DamageOut = 2x if right next to Liebhaber, scales down to x1.1 if far away
Liebhabers attacks heal you

Crush-Death Buff:
DamageOut 2x
Speed x1.5
DamageIn x0.8

## Stats
OutHeal +20%

# Star

## Ability: Spotlight
In an area around the Star every entity moves faster (MoveSpeed 1.5x).
Additionally, every buff inflicted by items carried by the Star also applies on everyone in the aura.

## Stats
MaxHealth -30%
DamageOut +30%
